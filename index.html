Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

CloserIQ is an AI-powered sales training app operated by Jacob Hayes-Farmer ("we", "us", "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at founder@closeriq.online.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following when you use CloserIQ:

  • Profile information you provide during onboarding (your name, industry, role, experience level, and earnings range)
  • Your responses to onboarding questions about your sales challenges and goals
  • Microphone audio during AI voice practice sessions, which is processed in real time by our voice AI provider (Vapi) and is not stored by us
  • Subscription and payment information, processed by Apple and not stored by us
  • Basic app usage data (sessions completed, features used) stored locally on your device

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Personalise your training plan and coaching tips
  • Enable AI voice roleplay sessions via the microphone
  • Process your subscription through Apple In-App Purchase
  • Improve the app and fix issues

4. Data Storage

Your onboarding answers and progress data are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account database. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Voice call audio is streamed directly to Vapi for real-time processing and is not recorded or retained by CloserIQ.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Vapi AI — real-time voice AI for practice calls. Audio is processed under Vapi's privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase — subscription billing. Payment data is handled by Apple.
  • Expo / EAS — app delivery infrastructure. No personal data is shared.

6. Microphone Access

CloserIQ requires microphone access to run AI voice training sessions. Audio is transmitted to Vapi for real-time speech recognition only. You can revoke microphone access at any time in your iPhone Settings, though this will disable voice practice sessions.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers listed above to the extent necessary to operate the app.

8. Children

CloserIQ is intended for users aged 17 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at founder@closeriq.online.

9. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your use of the app by contacting founder@closeriq.online. Since data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app removes all app data immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
